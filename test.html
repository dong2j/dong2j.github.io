Junjie Dong

 

I study planetary materials: the minerals, rocks, metals, and ices whose atomic-scale properties shape how planets form and change. My group recreates the pressures and temperatures of planetary interiors in the laboratory, pairing synchrotron experiments with atomistic and thermodynamic modeling, and uses what we learn to read the evidence from Earth and other planets.

We are currently curious about the thermodynamics of planetary materials, volatiles and the habitability of rocky planets, and the interiors of giant planets and exoplanets.
