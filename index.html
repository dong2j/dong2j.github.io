Junjie Dong

 

I am interested in planetary materials broadly, including minerals and rocks, metals, and ices. Their atomic scale properties control how planets form and evolve over time. My group recreates the extreme conditions of planetary interiors, combining high-pressure experiments at synchrotron sources with atomistic and thermodynamic modeling. These tools let us connect the physics and chemistry of materials to what we observe of Earth and other planets, geophysically, geochemically, and astronomically. My current interests are: Thermodynamics of planetary materials, Volatiles and the habitability of rocky planets, Interiors of giant planets and exoplanets


I am an assistant professor of geosciences at SUNY Stony Brook. You can reach me at junjie.dong@stonybrook.edu.
